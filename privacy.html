Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Max Decibel ("the Application") is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner for personal use. This policy explains what data the Application accesses and how it is handled.

What we access

With the owner's explicit authorization, the Application may access the owner's own Google account data through Google APIs, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Sheets, and Google Docs. It accesses only the account of the individual who granted access.

How data is used

Accessed data is used solely to provide personal assistant functionality to the owner — for example, summarizing email, checking calendar events, or organizing files. Data is used only on behalf of, and for the benefit of, the account owner.

Sharing

The Application does not sell, rent, or share accessed data with third parties. Data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials are stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner and are not shared. Access can be revoked at any time by the owner via their Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application owner.
